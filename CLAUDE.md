# NMMS Prep

An offline practice app (PWA) for the NMMS exam, both parts: the **Mental Ability Test (MAT)** and the **Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT)**, for Class 8 students (on phones) and teachers (on a classroom projector). The content comes from `sources/study-book/NMMS.pdf` (the Karnataka KSQAAC "Spardha Yashassu" 2022 study material), past NMMS papers, and the 2026-27 Class 7–8 textbooks for SAT. See [README.md](README.md) for what each screen does.

## Scope and product decisions

- **English and Kannada.** An EN / ಕನ್ನಡ switch covers the whole app, and **EN+ಕ** on chapter screens and the Shapes page shows questions, explanations and tips in both at once (`components/Bi.tsx`, `lib/i18n/both.ts`). All the Kannada is an unreviewed draft (see README, "Kannada"). Every new chapter needs `<topic>.kn.json` and `<topic>.meta.kn.json`, and generators build every sentence in both languages (`both()` in `lib/i18n/gen.ts`).
- **MAT is complete:** all 38 chapters are built, each in full (book questions, checker, generator, tips, Kannada) and in its own commit. Don't start on a backend or logins without asking.
- **Superimpose Figures** (chapter 39, `superimpose-figures`) is not in the book either, but past papers ask it: two or three figures put one on top of the other (sometimes one turned first). Like Miscellaneous it has no book questions, so it uses its past-paper questions (`pastOnly`), but it has a generator (`lib/generators/superimpose.ts`).
- **Missing Figures** (chapter 40, `missing-figures`) is the same kind of chapter: a figure with one part blank, to be completed from the options (`pattern: "fig-missing-part"`). Past-paper questions only, plus a generator (`lib/generators/missingPart.ts`).
- **Miscellaneous** (chapter 41, `miscellaneous`) is not in the book: past-paper MAT questions that fit no book chapter go there. It has no book questions and no generator, so Practice, Quick test, Classroom and Learn's worked examples use its past-paper questions instead (`pastOnly` in `data/topics.ts`). A question that fits a book chapter belongs in that chapter, not here.
- **SAT** (`/sat/<topic-id>`, Home's SAT tab at `/sat`): the 2026-27 Class 7–8 textbook chapters from `sources/sat-syllabus/*.md`: all 111: 29 maths, 25 science and 57 social science. For revision, related chapters are combined into topics (`data/sat/groups.json`, 80 topics); each topic page keeps the textbook reference (class, chapter, part, pages) for every chapter in it. All six Karnataka SAT papers (2019-20 to 2025-26) are transcribed in `data/pyq/ka-<year>-sat.json`, each question filed under a syllabus chapter ID (`topic`, or `null` if the new textbooks dropped it; those name an older-syllabus chapter instead, `oldChapter` from `data/sat/old-chapters.json`, shown as "Old syllabus – …" topics). Their Kannada, as printed in each paper, is in `ka-<year>-sat.kn.json` (the 2019-20 and 2020-21 PDFs store it as Nudi text: `tools/extract_nudi.swift` + `tools/nudi.ts`; the scanned papers were typed from the page images). **The maths and science are solved** (maths 119 of 120, science 206 of 210; the rest wait for an official key, see their notes): each answer has `keyFrom: "solved"` and a `solution` (the syllabus concept, cited by its section in `sources/sat-syllabus/*.md`, then the steps; Kannada in the `.kn.json`), and `tools/check_sat_maths.ts` and `tools/check_sat_science.ts` re-work every answer independently. A science question from an old-syllabus chapter has no section to cite, so its concept starts "Old syllabus – <chapter>:" (Kannada "ಹಳೆಯ ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ – …:"). SAT Practice asks every past question one at a time (MAT has no separate list of past questions either): picking an option on a solved one marks it and shows the solution, and the others are shown as printed. Don't solve social science unless asked. Re-run `tools/build_sat_chapters.ts` after changing papers or topics; `tools/check_sat.ts` is part of `check:content`. Don't read the textbook PDFs in `sources/sat-syllabus/textbooks/` (they are huge) except for a revision sheet; otherwise use the syllabus markdown.
- **SAT book questions** (`data/sat/book.json` + `.kn.json`, Practice's **Book** tab): the SAT questions from `NMMS.pdf` (PDF pp. 180–296), numbered per section as printed (ids `bkp-`, `bkc-`, `bkb-`, `bks-`, `bkm-`), each filed under a syllabus chapter, else an existing old-syllabus chapter, else the subject's **Miscellaneous** topic (`topic: null`; its solution concept starts "Beyond the syllabus:" / "ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮದ ಹೊರಗೆ:"). The book's key is checked, not trusted: science and maths answers get a solution and a rule in `check_sat_science.ts` / `check_sat_maths.ts`, social science a true/false rule in `check_sat_social.ts`; a wrong key is corrected (`keyFrom: "solved"`), and a question with no single right answer has no answer and a note. All 539 are in (physics 64, chemistry 70, biology 108, social science 145, maths 152). Figures are cropped by `tools/figures/sat-book.json`.
- **SAT revision sheets** (`/sat/<topic>/revise`, `pages/SatRevise.tsx`, listed in `data/satRevise.ts`): one page per textbook chapter with key terms, formulas with an example and the book page, and common mistakes, in `data/sat/revise/<chapter>.json` + `.kn.json`. Use the `textbook-extract` skill (`.claude/skills/`) to read the chapter's pages (many maths pages have no text layer, and OCR damages maths) and write in our own words: the books are marked "not to be republished", so copy no paragraphs, exercises or figures. `tools/check_sat_revise.ts` needs a rule for every formula and mistake. **Flash cards** (`/sat/<topic>/flashcards`, `pages/SatFlashcards.tsx`, `lib/flashcards.ts`) are made from the same sheet (each term and formula, not the mistakes), so they need no content of their own; each card's name must make sense as a prompt. Cover every chapter equally: questions can come from any chapter, so past-paper counts don't decide what goes in. All 111 chapters have sheets (29 maths, 25 science, 57 social science). A rule that can't be computed uses `read('p. N')`, which names the book page it was read from. A science sheet's "formula" is usually a fact in words, so most of its rules are `read`. The syllabus notes have wrong details (Venus's temperature, an acid the book only asks about), so check a sheet's facts against the book page. The social science books have their own errors (Columbus "around 1498", Scott called American, the Secretariat at Lake Success), so a sheet leaves out a fact the book contradicts or plainly gets wrong.
- **Mock exam** (`/mock`, `pages/MockExam.tsx`, `lib/mock.ts`, opened from the Mock exam button in every page's top bar, `components/MockLink.tsx`): MAT, SAT or both under one clock (a minute a question), all chapters or chosen ones, 15/45/90 questions per test, or a real MAT paper. MAT asks only book, past-paper and model-paper questions (no generated ones). SAT asks only answered questions: its social science comes from the study book, as the papers' has no key. One exam in progress at a time, in IndexedDB (`mock:current`, `mock:history`); leaving the page pauses it. **Print as a paper** (`/mock/print`, `pages/MockPrint.tsx`, `lib/mockPrint.ts`) prints the same choice for a class: question paper, answer sheet, answer key (+ solutions); the address carries a paper code that seeds `buildMock`, so it always rebuilds the same paper and key. Its print styles are the `.paper` rules and `@media print` at the end of `index.css`; the question paper hides SAT notes, which can name the answer.
- **Shapes and sizes** (`/shapes/<shape>/<step>`) is a maths visual beside the MAT chapters, not a chapter: perimeter, area, volume and nets of a square, rectangle, triangle and circle, with the solids drawn in 3D as plain SVG (`lib/solids.ts`, no 3D library). Its formulas and numbers live in `data/shapes.ts`, and `lib/solids.test.ts` checks them and each net's area.
- **Landing page** (`app/public/index.html`, the site's main URL `/`) and **About page** (`app/public/about/index.html`, at `/about/`: why the app was built, who made it) are plain HTML files, not app routes, so search engines read their text without running JavaScript. They share `public/page.css`, whose colours are copied from `index.css`, so change both. The app itself is `app/app.html`, with MAT Home at `/mat` and SAT Home at `/sat`; the landing page's links into the app go there. An inline script on the landing page sends old hash links (`/#/t/…`) and the installed app (whose start page used to be `/`) on to `/mat`. The old `/welcome/` redirects to `/` (`worker.ts`). Home links to About. Both work offline through the service worker's precache and are English-only. `src/landing.test.ts` checks the landing page's numbers and tool links against the app's data. Update `dateModified` and `lastmod` in `public/sitemap.xml` when their text changes.
- **The theme starts on Light**, even on phones set to dark mode, Every page has a settings button (`components/SettingsMenu.tsx`) that opens the language and Light / Dark / Auto theme switches: on Home it is a chip showing the current language and theme ("EN | ☀️ ▾"), elsewhere an **Aa** button. Classroom projectors need light.
- **Home is a compact list** (one row per chapter); each row opens the chapter page, where **Learn is the highlighted (blue) button**. The ← back arrow on Learn/Practice/Test/Classroom/Rule goes to Home.
- **The pass mark is shown simply as 40%**.
- **Clues** (`components/ClueCard.tsx`, `lib/clues.ts`): step-by-step clues, one at a time in a card under the figure or matrix, in Practice and Classroom only (never in Quick test or Mock exam). Every chapter you work something out in has them (29 chapters). The picture chapters solved by eye have none, because a clue there would just say where to look: Analogy of Figures, Figure Series, Hidden, Similar and Intersecting Figures, Folding a Sheet, Paper Punch, Mirror and Water Images, Superimpose, Missing Figures and Miscellaneous. They live in `data/clues/<topic>.json` + `.kn.json` by question id (book and past-paper questions; needs-review ones get none). The generators for Figures and Number Relationship, Number Matrix and Age Problems add their own; the other chapters' generated questions have no clues yet. Clues go from where to look, to the rule shown on a complete figure, to the missing one set up but not worked out: the last clue never gives the answer. `tools/check_clues.ts` (in `check:content`) checks this. A new chapter's clues file is listed in `CLUE_FILES`.
- **Warm-up** (Practice's first tab, `/mat/<topic>/practice/warmup`): a round of 10 generated questions that build the chapter's foundations before the book questions, easiest step first, with clues. A chapter opts in with `warmup` in `data/topics.ts`. So far only Age Problems (`lib/generators/ageWarmup.ts`: time line, gap and sum, words to an expression, sentence to an equation; its test compares expressions and equations as functions of x, not as text).
- **Difficulty levels** (`lib/levels.ts`): every chapter ranks its questions 1 (easy) to 4 (hardest) by the steps their rule takes (for picture chapters, judged by eye from the figures), in `data/levels/<topic>.json` by question id, with the meaning of each level in its `_note`. Practice (Book and Past exams) and Classroom ask them easiest first, keeping the order within a level, and show the level as a chip ("Easy"). `tools/check_levels.ts` (in `check:content`) needs a level for every question students see and a levels file for every chapter. A new chapter's file is listed in `LEVEL_FILES`.

## Checks (run in `app/`)

Before finishing a change, run `npm test`, `npm run check:content`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build`. For UI changes, also check the page in a browser at 360px width in light and dark. There must be no sideways page scroll and no console errors.

## Deployment

- **Live at https://mystudyprep.in/** on Cloudflare Workers (`app/wrangler.jsonc`, static assets from `app/dist`); the domain is registered at Hostinger with Cloudflare's nameservers. Public repo `valevoor/nmms-prep`.
- **Pushing to `main` deploys.** `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs lint, tests, `check:content` and build, then runs `wrangler deploy` (repo secrets `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`, `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID`).
- **The old address** (https://valevoor.github.io/nmms-prep/) is retired: GitHub Pages serves only `github-pages-redirect/` (its own workflow), a page that redirects every old path to mystudyprep.in and a `sw.js` that removes the old app's offline copy. Don't publish the app there again.
- **Talk slides:** `slides/nmms-exam-overview.md` is a Marp deck. The workflow renders it to `dist/slides/` (live at `/slides/`), and `navigateFallbackDenylist` in `vite.config.ts` keeps the app's service worker away from it.
- **Before you push:** a push goes live for students within minutes, so run the checks locally first. Only commit or push when the user asks.
- **Paths:** the app uses path routes (`/mat/number-series/learn`), so it is served from the site root (`base: '/'` in `vite.config.ts`, which builds `app.html`). Files are served as they are; for every other path the Worker script (`app/worker.ts`, `main` in `wrangler.jsonc`) answers with `app.html`, as does the service worker offline (`navigateFallback`), and the `site-pages` plugin in `vite.config.ts` does the same for `npm run dev` and `npm run preview`. Test Worker changes locally with `npx wrangler dev` after a build. `<base href="/">` in `app.html` makes relative asset paths (`figures/…`) start from the root, so keep them relative to it. Old hash links (`/#/t/…`) still work: `pathFromHash` in `lib/router.ts` turns them into paths.

## Workflows

- **Adding a MAT chapter:** follow the `add-mat-chapter` skill (`.claude/skills/add-mat-chapter/SKILL.md`). Every chapter needs book questions, a checker, a generator, levels, clues (unless solved by eye) and Kannada.
- **Adding a past paper:** follow the `add-past-paper` skill (`.claude/skills/add-past-paper/SKILL.md`). Papers have no key, so every answer is `keyFrom: "solved"` and needs an independent rule.

## Model papers

Practice papers made by an education office, not real exams, are in `sources/question-papers/model/` and go into `data/model/<paper>.json` + `.kn.json`, listed in `MODEL_FILES` (`data/papers.ts`). Their questions carry `model`, not `paper`, so they get no "Real exam" chip and stay out of Past exams, a real paper in the Mock exam and the Home counts; they show in Practice's own **Model papers** tab and Classroom's **Model papers** list, and the Mock exam's mixed papers draw on them with the book and past-paper questions (chapter shares are still weighted by past papers). Each printed section is numbered from Q1 as printed (`paperNo`) and can feed more than one chapter, so every question has its own `topic`. A question that repeats a book or past-paper question (same terms or table and options, or the same series with a term added) is left out and listed in its section's `dropped` with the id it repeats. The paper's key is checked, not trusted: `tools/check_model.ts` (in `check:content`) needs a rule for every question students see, fails on a repeat, and for odd-one-out on single numbers flags an option that is the only prime, square or cube; doubtful questions are `needs-review` with a note and listed in its `UNSURE`. Imported: Bengaluru North daily papers, all 10 sections (173 questions in 12 chapters, 9 hidden, 4 repeats dropped). They have no levels or clues yet, so the tab asks them in printed order.

## Content rules

- **The book's answer keys are incomplete and sometimes wrong** (numbering drifts, typos). Never trust a key without checking it.
- **`keyFrom`:** set `"book"` when the answer is from the book's key, and `"solved"` when we worked it out. The app shows which one to students.
- **Odd one out:** if another obvious property (prime, square…) makes a *different* option the odd one, the question has two answers. Mark it `needs-review` with a note, as for Ch 18 Q2 and Q22. A difference that is only odd/even doesn't count.
- **Unclear questions:** if a question can't be resolved, set `"status": "needs-review"` with a `note`. It is hidden from students. Number Series Q22 is in this state, pending a check against the original paper. Don't guess a fix.
- **Duplicate questions** in the book are dropped.
- **Generators must never produce two correct options.** For "Guess the rule", every wrong option is checked against the series or pair (see `fitsPattern` and `fitsAnalogy`), and questions that two rules could explain are skipped.

## Code conventions

- **Question data:** keep content in JSON or TypeScript data files, not inside components. Questions of every kind share the `Question` type in `types.ts` (`kind`: missing / rule / wrong; `layout`: series / analogy).
- **Styles:** all colours are CSS variables in `index.css`. The dark theme is `:root[data-theme='dark']`, so new styles must use the variables and look right in both themes.
- **MAT and SAT screens match:** each SAT screen has a MAT counterpart (Home's two tabs: `ChapterRow` ↔ `SatRow`; `Chapter` ↔ `SatTopic`; `Practice` + `QuestionStem` + `Options` ↔ `SatPractice` + `SatQuestion`). Make a UI change on both sides, reusing the MAT classes and components rather than adding `sat-*` copies. `app/src/parity.test.tsx` renders both sides and fails on any class used on only one side unless it is listed there with a reason; a reason starting "Drift:" is a known difference to remove. A Claude Code hook (`.claude/settings.json` → `tools/ui_pair_hint.mjs`) names the counterpart after each edit to a paired file. A new SAT screen gets a pair in the test.
- **Phones first:** tap targets of at least 44–48px, text sized for Class 8 readers, and screen-reader labels on icon-only controls.
- **Offline:** everything must work offline after the first visit, so don't load anything from the network at runtime (no CDN fonts or remote APIs).
- **Visit counts:** Cloudflare Web Analytics only, enabled in the Cloudflare dashboard (its beacon is injected at the edge; there is no tracker code in the repo). No cookies, no personal data. Don't add other trackers.
