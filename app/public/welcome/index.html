Learn by doing

Try the hard topics with your own hands.

  • Try a mirror or water: type letters or place shapes, and see their image
  • Fold and punch: fold a sheet, cut holes, then open it out to see where they land
  • Draw and fold: draw on a see-through sheet and watch it fold over in 3D
  • Fold the dice: fold an open dice into a cube and see which faces are opposite
  • Cut the cube: slice a cube in 3D and count the pieces
  • Shapes and sizes: see perimeter, area, volume and nets step by step

Questions and answers

What is NMMS Prep?

An app for practising the two NMMS papers, MAT and SAT. NMMS is a Government of India scholarship worth ₹12,000 a year from Class 9 to Class 12.

How does it help with the MAT?

It covers 41 chapters of reasoning. You get practice questions with explanations, past exam questions and a quick test in each. Most chapters make new questions whenever you run out, and answers from the book and past papers are checked, but mistakes are still possible.

How does it help with the SAT?

It groups maths, science and social science into 66 revision topics, with 540 past exam questions and the textbook pages to study. Maths and science have worked solutions. Social science is shown as printed, without answers for now.

Is it free? Does it work offline?

It is free, with no sign-up. After your first visit it works without internet, and you can add it to your phone's home screen.

Is it up to date?

Yes. The SAT topics follow the 2026-27 textbooks, and the past papers go up to 2025-26.

Can students from any state use it?

Yes. MAT question types are much the same across states. SAT topics and past papers come from Karnataka, so check them against your own state's syllabus. The app is in English and Kannada, and some of the Kannada is still being reviewed.
