About NMMS Prep

Why this app was built

For students

Most NMMS students come from low-income families, often with no computer and only a shared phone with limited internet. Many have no access to NMMS preparation, at home or at school. This app aims to be their self-study companion.

For teachers

Material to learn each topic yourself and teach it in class.

Who made this app?

Vishwanath, an NMMS teaching volunteer, developed this app. For feedback or support, you can reach Vishwanath on LinkedIn.
