Summary

Most questions are fine: the findings cover of the questions checked. The ones that matter most for students are the wrong key rows, because a student who works the question correctly will be marked wrong.

Kinds of finding

Wrong key

The key’s answer is wrong. The correct option is given, with the working.

No single answer

Two options are right, none is, or the question or figure can’t be read as printed. The question needs rewording.

Key missing

The key gives no answer. A worked answer is given.

Debatable

The key can be defended, but another option fits better, or the fact behind it is disputed or out of date.

Misprint

A typo, a mislabelled option, a mismatch between language versions or a repeated question. The key’s answer stands.
